## Why we asked

Plenty of people tell us that work gives their life meaning, and people who work do report a stronger sense of purpose than people who do not. That finding treats a job as one thing, though, and jobs differ enormously. A job can be varied or repetitive, lonely or full of good colleagues. We wanted to know which of those features are linked to feeling that your life has direction, and not only to feeling that your work is meaningful.

## What we did

We used the Midlife in the United States study (MIDUS), which surveyed adults in 1994-1995, 2003-2004 and 2013. Our sample was the 4,963 people who answered the questions we needed at least twice. We looked at four work characteristics: skill variety (using many different skills), autonomy, coworker support and supervisor support. Purpose came from a three-item scale. We also asked whether the links depended on work-home spillover, meaning how much stress or good feeling carries between the office and home. We preregistered the analyses and report our deviations in the paper.

## What we found

At the same point in time, people with more skill variety and more coworker support reported a stronger sense of purpose. Autonomy and supervisor support were not uniquely associated with purpose once the other characteristics were in the model. Across the roughly ten years between waves, people whose work started out with more skill variety tended to show larger increases in purpose. Spillover did not change any of these links.

The associations were modest, in line with other studies of change in purpose. The three-item purpose scale also had low reliability (alphas of about .29 to .35), so we treat these estimates as rough. In an exploratory analysis, higher purpose at the start was associated with later increases in autonomy and coworker support, so the arrow could run in both directions.

## What it means

Varied, challenging work and friendly colleagues go along with a stronger sense of direction. Our data are observational, and we cannot say that redesigning a job would raise anyone's purpose, though it would be a reasonable thing to test.

Work is one source of purpose among several.
