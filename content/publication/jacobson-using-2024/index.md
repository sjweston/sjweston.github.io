## What we did

Narrative identity research asks people to tell stories about their lives. Reading and coding those stories by hand takes a long time, and it usually relies on coding systems chosen in advance. We wanted to see what structural topic modeling (STM), a text-analysis method that finds clusters of words that tend to appear together, could add. In our preregistered study, 1,149 ethnically diverse young adults wrote about a moment when they felt aware of their ethnicity.

## What we found

STM identified 14 topics in the narratives. We then asked how each topic related to the writer's ethnicity and to two themes that human coders had already rated, agency and communion. In other words, we compared what the algorithm found with what people found. Some topics lined up with those themes. For example, a topic we labeled Gained perspective of structural inequality was associated with greater agency, and Peer dynamics was associated with greater communion. Ethnic/cultural celebration was associated with both.

Pairing STM with human coding gave us greater insight into the content of ethnic experiences than either would alone.
