## What this paper is about

This is a short report written with colleagues in dermatology, oncology, and surgery at Washington University in St. Louis. By its title, it looks at health-related quality of life in patients with malignant melanoma, compared by stage of disease and by treatment status.

The paper is not openly available and has no abstract in the databases we checked, so we have not summarized its findings here. Readers who want the results can find them through the publisher's page linked above.
